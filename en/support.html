Support — Madocal

日本語

Madocal puts a calendar with a photo or color background in widgets on your Home Screen and Lock Screen, so you can check your events without opening the app.

FAQ

Q. What can I buy?
A. Only “Madocal PRO” (one-time purchase). The calendar, adding events, the widgets and the notification about today’s events are free. Madocal PRO lets you use photos and wallpapers as backgrounds for the app and the widgets, lets you change fonts and holiday colors, and removes ads.

Q. I bought PRO, but it is not unlocked.
A. Go to Settings → “Restore Purchases”. Please also check that you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used for the purchase.

Q. Can it show events from my iPhone’s Calendar?
A. Turn on sync on the “Sync with iPhone Calendar” screen in Settings to show events from your iPhone’s Calendar in Madocal and its widgets. If you have added other calendar accounts to your iPhone’s Calendar, their events can be shown too.

Q. Are my events sent anywhere?
A. Events saved in the app are stored only on your iPhone and are never sent to the developer’s servers.
