Privacy Policy — Madocal

Last updated: 2026-11-22 · 日本語

1. Overview

Madocal (the “App”) does not collect information that directly identifies you, such as your name, email address or phone number. There is no account registration.

The App communicates outside your device only for the following:

  • The App Store (purchases, restoring purchases and checking prices; handled by Apple; see “7. In-App Purchase”)
  • Data needed to deliver third-party ads (Google AdMob), only if you have not purchased Madocal PRO (see “6. Ads”)
  • App usage data, only if you agree (see “4. Sending usage data”)
  • What you enter when you open the feedback form from Settings (see “10. Feedback form”)

2. Events and calendars

Events you add in the App are stored, by default, only on this iPhone (in a storage area shared by the App and its widgets) and are never sent out.

The App asks for access to your iPhone’s Calendar only if you turn on “Sync with iPhone Calendar” in Settings. If you allow access, the App shows events from your iPhone’s Calendar in the App and its widgets, and saves new events to your iPhone’s Calendar. Events of accounts you added to your iPhone’s Calendar are synced by iOS and the service of that account. The App never sends events to the developer’s servers.

For events in your iPhone’s Calendar, the App stores only pairs of an event identifier and the color you chose.

3. Photos

Background photos are chosen with the iOS photo picker. The App does not ask for access to your whole photo library. Photos you choose are resized and stored on this iPhone, and are never sent out.

4. Sending usage data

When you first open the App, we ask whether you want to send usage data. Only if you choose to send it is usage data sent to Google’s analytics service (Firebase Analytics).

What is sent

  • Which pages of the first-launch guide you reached and whether you skipped the guide
  • Whether you saved an event in the App for the first time
  • Whether you opened the purchase screen (and from which screen) and the result of a purchase
  • Whether you tapped “Restore Purchases”
  • How many times you opened the App and how long you used it
  • iPhone model, iOS version and App version
  • Language, time of day and approximate region
  • An identifier used only by this App

What is not sent

  • The contents of your events (titles, dates and times, notes) and the names of your calendars
  • Photos and background images

The purpose is to count how far people get in the App and where they stop, so we can fix what is hard to use. This data is not used for ad delivery or targeting. The recipient is Google LLC, and the data is handled under the Firebase Data Processing Terms. The App uses this service only under these terms, which require protection equivalent to this policy. For details, see Privacy and Security in Firebase.

You can change this at any time in the App under Settings → “Send Usage Data”. Your choice does not affect purchases or any features.

5. Information stored only on your device

Events saved in the App, background photos and settings (widget designs, app backgrounds, first day of the week, holiday display, sync and notifications on/off, notification time, sending usage data) are stored only on this iPhone and are never sent out. They are included in your iOS device backups. They are erased when you delete the App.

6. Ads

If you have not purchased Madocal PRO, the App shows banner ads from Google AdMob. To deliver ads, Google may collect and use data such as device identifiers, approximate location (based on IP address) and interactions with ads. For details, see Google’s policies. The App shows ads only under Google’s terms, which require protection equivalent to this policy.

The App does not ask for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency), so the advertising identifier (IDFA) stays unavailable. After you purchase Madocal PRO, no ads are shown.

7. In-App Purchase

The only purchase in the App is “Madocal PRO” (one-time, non-consumable). Payment is processed by Apple’s App Store, and the App never receives payment information such as card numbers. Purchase status is checked on your device from Apple’s transaction information each time and is not stored separately by the App.

8. Notifications

Only if you turn on “Notify About Today’s Events” in Settings, the App schedules notifications about the day’s events on this iPhone. The contents of notifications never leave your device.

9. Retention and withdrawing consent

You can turn off sending usage data at any time in Settings → “Send Usage Data”. After you turn it off, nothing more is sent.

Individual records that were already sent are deleted when the Google Analytics data retention period ends. Aggregated numbers that do not identify anyone remain in reports. Because the developer cannot tell whose records they are, we cannot find and delete them individually. Questions about deletion are accepted through GitHub Issues on the Support page.

10. Feedback form

Tapping “Report Issues & Request Features” in Settings opens a Google Form. What you enter and the app name you choose are sent to Google and read by the developer. Email addresses are not collected.

11. Contact

For questions about this policy, see the Support page.
